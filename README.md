What I do
At a major commercial bank in China, I lead an internal AI orchestration platform used across the organisation. It gives product and engineering teams one shared way to build, deploy and govern AI applications, instead of every team rebuilding the same foundations.
- Workflow orchestration — a node-based execution engine for multi-step LLM pipelines
- Agent mode — tool-calling agents with bounded, auditable execution
- Reusable components and templates — shared patterns so teams don't start from zero
- Deployment tooling — packaging, release and rollout for AI applications
- Governance layer — the guardrails that make AI acceptable inside a regulated bank
I spent my first year as a hands-on Python developer on the orchestration and agent-execution layers, then moved into leading the platform's product design and delivery.
